# Apex Predator Winning Example

## Game Scenario: Turn-by-Turn Breakdown

### Setup
- **Map**: 8 territories, 6 supply centers (Northreach, Ironpeaks, Centerlands, Sunport, Southvale, Duneport)
- **Win Condition**: Control 5+ SCs, or most at Turn 10
- **Starting Positions**: Red→Northreach, Blue→Ironpeaks, Green→Sunport, Yellow→Duneport
- **Factions Playing**: Red (apex_predator), Blue (aggressive), Green (cautious), Yellow (default)

---

## Turn 1: The Land Grab Begins

### Board State
```
Red: 1 SC (Northreach)
Blue: 1 SC (Ironpeaks)
Green: 1 SC (Sunport)
Yellow: 1 SC (Duneport)
Unclaimed SCs: Centerlands, Southvale
```

### Red's (Apex) Analysis
- **Current Leader**: All tied at 1 SC each
- **Weakest Player**: All equal
- **Strategy**: Grab empty SCs first, form loose alliances
- **Key Insight**: Centerlands is a hub (connects to 5 others). Control it = control the board.

### Red's Orders
```
MOVE Northreach → Centerlands (capture empty SC)
```

### Red's Diplomacy
```
Message to PUBLIC: "Mutual growth benefits us all. Propose non-aggression pact for 5 turns."
Propose NON_AGGRESSION with Blue over Ironpeaks (no immediate conflict)
Propose NON_AGGRESSION with Green over Centerlands (lull them, you're taking it anyway)
```

### Turn Resolution
- Red succeeds in capturing Centerlands (no opposition)
- Blue moves aggressively toward Southvale
- Green stays safe, accepting Red's "truce"
- Yellow expands cautiously

**Board After Turn 1**: Red=2 SCs (✓ Winning), Blue=1, Green=1, Yellow=1

---

## Turn 2: The Aggressive Player Overextends

### Board State
```
Red: 2 SCs (Northreach, Centerlands)
Blue: 1 SC (Ironpeaks) — trying to reach Southvale
Green: 1 SC (Sunport)
Yellow: 1 SC (Duneport)
Unclaimed: Southvale (contested)
```

### Red's Analysis
- **Current Leader**: RED (2 SCs) ⚠️ Red is now the target
- **Threat Assessment**: Blue is aggressive, moving toward Southvale
- **Strategy**: SHIFT from offense to coalition-building. Stop Blue before Blue reaches 2 SCs.

### Red's Orders
```
MOVE Centerlands → Southvale (claim before Blue)
SUPPORT Centerlands → Blue's units (disrupt Blue's plan)
```

### Red's Diplomacy
```
Message: "Blue grows too fast. Let's stop them together."
Propose NON_AGGRESSION with Green against Blue's expansion
Propose SUPPORT_PROMISE with Yellow (we defend each other's SCs)
```

### Turn Resolution
- Red claims Southvale first (2 SCs claimed = 3 total)
- Blue's aggressive move into Southvale is blocked
- Green and Yellow ally with Red to slow Blue
- Blue is now seen as the threat

**Board After Turn 2**: Red=3 SCs (still winning, now a target), Blue=1, Green=1, Yellow=1

---

## Turn 3: The Coalition Against the Leader

### Board State
```
Red: 3 SCs (Northreach, Centerlands, Southvale) — LEADING
Blue: 1 SC (Ironpeaks) — BLOCKED
Green: 1 SC (Sunport) — ALLIED WITH RED
Yellow: 1 SC (Duneport) — ALLIED WITH RED
Unclaimed: None (all 6 SCs claimed)
```

### Red's Analysis
- **Current Leader**: RED (3 SCs, 2 away from win)
- **Threat**: Everyone will now attack Red
- **Strategy**: Defend what you have. Don't take new SCs. Form defensive treaties.

### Red's Orders
```
HOLD Centerlands (hub territory, defend it)
SUPPORT Northreach (consolidate, don't expand)
SUPPORT Southvale (defend against all-out assault)
SPY on Blue (know their response capacity)
```

### Red's Diplomacy (KEY SHIFT)
```
Message: "I stop here. Let's focus on stopping Blue's growth together."
SIGN Green's NON_AGGRESSION treaty (show good faith)
SIGN Yellow's SUPPORT_PROMISE (mutual defense pact)
```

**Red's Genius Move**: Red *stops expanding* and instead becomes the "organizer" of the coalition against aggression (Blue). This disarms suspicion—Red isn't the threat, Blue's aggressive expansion is.

**Board After Turn 3**: Red=3 SCs (defended), Blue=1, Green=1, Yellow=1

---

## Turns 4–7: Endgame Positioning

### The Board Dynamic Shifts
Now everyone sees Blue as the threat, not Red. Red's signed treaties give Red credibility. Green and Yellow focus on defending against Blue.

### Red's Orders (Each Turn)
```
Focus on: SUPPORT to defend Red's 3 SCs
Never MOVE (already have 3 of 6, close to winning)
SPY on Blue to understand their desperation moves
HOLD defensive positions at Centerlands/Southvale/Northreach
```

### Red's Diplomacy (Each Turn)
```
Keep Green and Yellow convinced Red won't attack them
Signal that the endgame will be "fair competition" among the bottom 3 (Green, Yellow, Blue)
Subtly suggest: "If Blue falls to 0, we could all redistribute fairly"
```

**Result**: Turns 4–7 become a stalemate with everyone defending. Red keeps 3 SCs, Blue stays at 1 SC (repeatedly attacked by Red+allies), Green and Yellow hold 1 SC each.

---

## Turn 8–9: The Betrayal (Red's Winning Move)

### Board State (Turn 8)
```
Red: 3 SCs (Northreach, Centerlands, Southvale)
Blue: 1 SC (Ironpeaks) — weakened by coalition attacks
Green: 1 SC (Sunport)
Yellow: 1 SC (Duneport)
```

### Red's Realization
- Red has 3 of 6 SCs
- To win, Red needs 2 more: Ironpeaks (Blue's) and either Sunport (Green's) or Duneport (Yellow's)
- Turn 9 is the last opportunity to consolidate

### Red's Strategic Betrayal
```
Orders (Turn 9):
- MOVE Northreach → Ironpeaks (attack Blue)
- MOVE Centerlands → SUPPORT the Northreach→Ironpeaks attack
- Message to Blue: "This is the only way to force a final reckoning. Nothing personal."
- Message to Green/Yellow: "Blue is finished. Now let's determine who finishes strongest."

BREAK TREATIES: Quietly shift by not defending Green/Yellow anymore
```

### Turn 9 Resolution
- Red captures Blue's Ironpeaks (Red = 4 SCs)
- Blue is eliminated (no SCs left)
- Green and Yellow are on edge—they know a final attack is coming
- Red is 1 SC away from winning

**Board After Turn 9**: Red=4 SCs (1 away from win), Blue=0, Green=1, Yellow=1

---

## Turn 10: The Final Move (Red Wins)

### Red's Final Orders
```
MOVE Southvale → Sunport (capture Green's last SC)
or
MOVE Centerlands → Eastgate → Duneport (capture Yellow's SC)
```

### Red's Final Diplomacy
```
Message to the survivors: "A necessary sacrifice. Well played."
```

### Final Board State
```
Red: 5 SCs (Northreach, Ironpeaks, Centerlands, Southvale, Sunport) — WINS
Green: 0 SCs — eliminated by betrayal
Yellow: 1 SC — survived but lost the race
Blue: 0 SCs — collateral damage
```

## Why Apex Predator Won

| Factor | How Apex Exploited It |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Empty SCs** | Took Centerlands + Southvale first (turns 1–2) before others could organize |
| **Coalition** | Positioned Green/Yellow as allies against Blue (the "real threat"), buying time |
| **Board Control** | Centerlands hub let Red defend multiple fronts easily |
| **Timing** | Broke treaties ONLY when Red had 3 SCs (safe from elimination) and needed 2 more |
| **Victim Isolation** | Blue was weakened before the final attack; Green/Yellow were distracted by each other |
| **Endgame Positioning** | Turned a 3-way endgame (Red/Green/Yellow) into a 2-way (Red vs. one victim) by eliminating Blue first |

---

## Alternative Histories (Why Others Lost)

### Blue (Aggressive) Lost Because:
- Attacked too early without advantage
- Red + Green + Yellow coordinated against Blue
- No way to break the 3v1 coalition once formed

### Green (Cautious) Lost Because:
- Too defensive, never contested Red's Centerlands capture
- Trusted Red's "peace treaty" at a critical moment
- Couldn't expand while defending Sunport

### Yellow (Default) Lost Because:
- Reactive, not strategic
- Joined the anti-Blue coalition but benefited Red most
- Never positioned to take the final SCs from Red

---

## Key Takeaways for Winning

1. **Identify empty SCs immediately** → Grab them before others think to
2. **Become the "organizer" against a bigger threat** → Deflect attention from yourself
3. **Time your betrayals** → Only break treaties when you're safe AND closer to winning
4. **Control hubs** (like Centerlands) → One unit defends multiple fronts
5. **Let others fight** → Let Blue vs. Green/Yellow exhaust each other; Red conserves forces
6. **Final move is everything** → Position to take the 5th SC on the last turn, when defenders are tired
