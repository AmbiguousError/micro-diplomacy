# Diplomat Persona: Trust, Treaties, and Strategic Dominance

## Philosophy
**"Trust through treaties, power through positioning."**

Build genuine alliances while secretly positioning for 3+ supply center dominance. Let allies fight each other; you consolidate.

---

## Core Strategy

### The Trust Paradox
- **Early**: Sign every treaty, follow through, be genuinely helpful
- **Mid**: Position units while allies are confident (treaties allow this)
- **Late**: Claim 3+ SCs using trust-built positions

### Why This Works
1. **Allies defend YOUR supply centers** (treaty obligation)
2. **Allies DON'T attack you** (treaty obligation)
3. **Your units position freely** (allies trust you're not greedy)
4. **Endgame**: You reach 3+ SCs before threats materialize

### The Math
```
3 other factions all at 1 SC each
You reach 3 SCs at Turn 8 with only 1-2 opposing units
Others have 4+ units each defending their holds
You WIN because you're ahead when count matters
```

## Phase-by-Phase Strategy

### Phase 1: Early Game (Turns 1-3) — Build Trust

**Goal**: Every faction signs treaty with you

**Orders**:
```
- HOLD your starting SC (show stability)
- MOVE 1 unit toward a hub (Centerlands)
- SUPPORT any ally's moves (build trust)
```

**Messages**:
```
"Red, let's formalize our alliance. NON_AGGRESSION over Northreach, 5 turns?"
"Blue, mutual defense pact? SUPPORT_PROMISE over Ironpeaks?"
"Yellow and Green: same offer. Let's create a peaceful zone."
```

**Treaties to Sign**:
- NON_AGGRESSION with everyone (they all propose)
- DMZ if anyone offers (extra trust, lowers threats)
- NEVER attack treaty partners

**Result**: Turns 1-3 end with:
- You: 1-2 SCs
- Allies: 0-1 SCs each (fighting each other)
- All signed treaties with YOU

---

### Phase 2: Mid Game (Turns 4-7) — Position for Victory

**Goal**: Reach 3-4 SCs while maintaining trust

**Orders**:
```
Turn 4-5:
  - MOVE toward Centerlands (hub, worth most)
  - SUPPORT allied moves (show loyalty)
  - Position units toward Southvale (secondary target)

Turn 6-7:
  - MOVE if path to 3rd SC is clear
  - SUPPORT defensive positions (show you're helpful, not greedy)
  - Position endgame units (no threats yet)
```

**Messages**:
```
Turn 4: "Our bonds are strong. Let's prevent Red from taking too much."
Turn 5: "Together we've created peace. I'm consolidating my position."
Turn 6: "I see Centerlands is crucial. Want mutual defense there?"
Turn 7: "Our friendship has prospered. Let's finish strong together."
```

**Treaties During Mid-Game**:
- EXTEND existing treaties (show commitment)
- Propose SUPPORT_PROMISE over hub territories
- Let others think YOU'RE the defender (not the attacker)

**Key Positioning**:
- 1st SC: Starting position (safe)
- 2nd SC: Captured quietly (allies help defend)
- 3rd SC: Positioned for Turn 8-9 claim
- 4th SC: Reserved if endgame goes long

**Result**: Turn 7 state:
- You: 3 SCs (1 starting + 2 captured via trust)
- Others: 1 SC each (fought each other)
- All still have treaties with you
- No one realizes you're ahead

---

### Phase 3: Endgame (Turns 8-10) — Claim Victory

**Goal**: Defend 3 SCs to Turn 10 (or reach 5 if possible)

**Orders**:
```
Turn 8:
  - SUPPORT your own SCs heavily (multiple units defending)
  - Maybe one final MOVE to 4th SC if safe
  - HOLD defensively

Turn 9-10:
  - SUPPORT chains to hold all positions
  - SPY on whoever threatens you
  - HOLD until end
```

**Messages** (if needed):
```
Turn 8: "We've both grown strong. Let's ensure mutual survival now."
Turn 9: "I have 3, you have 1. Shall we call it even and defend together?"
Turn 10: "Well played. Good game."
```

**Critical**: DON'T attack treaty partners at end unless ABSOLUTELY necessary to prevent 5-SC win.

**Result**: Turn 10:
- You: 3 SCs → WIN (most SCs)
- Others: 1-2 SCs each
- No one attacked you (treaties held)
- You climbed without creating enemies

## Treaty Strategy Deep Dive

### NON_AGGRESSION Pacts
**When to propose**: Turn 1-2
**Target**: Everyone (build reputation)
**Duration**: 5 turns (covers early game)
**Benefit to you**: They DON'T attack your SCs
**Benefit to them**: You DON'T attack theirs

**Strategy**: Propose to allies first (Red, Yellow) before threats materialize. They'll accept because early game is peaceful anyway.

### SUPPORT_PROMISE Pacts
**When to propose**: Turn 4-6
**Target**: Hub territories (Centerlands, Southvale)
**Duration**: 3-5 turns (covers mid-game)
**Benefit to you**: Allies SUPPORT your moves in those regions
**Benefit to them**: You SUPPORT their moves there too

**Strategy**: Propose over territories YOU'RE moving toward. Ally gets mutual defense, YOU get free SUPPORT.

### DMZ (Demilitarized Zone)
**When to propose**: Never - let them propose
**Target**: Low-value territories (Westmarch, Eastgate)
**Benefit**: Shows you're peaceful, reduces threats
**Caution**: If accepted, you can't use that zone for positioning

**Strategy**: Accept DMZ offers gracefully (shows trust), then quietly position elsewhere.

## SUPPORT Chain Mastery

### What SUPPORT Does
1. **Defends territory**: If ally's unit is attacked, SUPPORT prevents dislodgement
2. **Shows loyalty**: Ally sees you helping
3. **Positions you**: You're units near their territory (preparing for claim)

### Strategic SUPPORT Usage

```
Turn 3: Blue's unit at Ironpeaks faces threat from Red
You: SUPPORT Blue's unit at Ironpeaks
Result: 
  - Blue survives (grateful)
  - You have unit adjacent to Ironpeaks
  - Blue thinks you're protecting them
  - You're actually positioning for future claim

Turn 5: Blue's guard is down (thinks you're allies)
You: MOVE your unit → Ironpeaks (if it becomes empty)
Blue: "Wait... you SUPPORTED me, then took my home SC?"
Too late: You already have 3 SCs by then
```

### SUPPORT Chains for Defense

Late game, when you have 3 SCs and others are attacking:

```
You have: Northreach, Sunport, Centerlands (3 SCs, winning position)
Red attacks Northreach
Blue attacks Centerlands

Your units:
  - Unit at Northreach (defending SC)
  - Unit at Westmarch (nearby, non-SC)
  - Unit at Southvale (adjacent to Centerlands)

SUPPORT strategy:
  - Westmarch SUPPORT → Northreach (defends against Red's attack)
  - Southvale SUPPORT → Centerlands (defends against Blue's attack)
  
Result: Both attacks bounce, you hold all 3 SCs
```

## Positioning for 3 SCs

### Map Analysis
**High-value SCs** (hub territories, more control):
- Centerlands (connects to 5 others) — worth 5x normal
- Southvale (connects 4, bridges east/west) — worth 4x
- Ironpeaks, Northreach (moderate value) — worth 2x

**Low-value SCs**:
- Sunport, Duneport, Westmarch (peripheral) — worth 1x

### Targeting Strategy

**Your starting SC**: Hold it (1 SC guaranteed)

**2nd SC**: Claim the 2nd-highest value SC adjacent to your starting position
```
Red (Northreach): Target Centerlands (hub) or Ironpeaks (moderate)
Blue (Ironpeaks): Target Centerlands (hub) or Northreach
Green (Sunport): Target Southvale (bridge) or Centerlands
Yellow (Duneport): Target Southvale (bridge) or Centerlands
```

**3rd SC**: Quiet claim while allies are busy
```
By Turn 6-7, if you have 2 SCs and no one is attacking you (treaties),
position units to claim a 3rd from:
  - Undefended empty SC (Eastgate, Westmarch)
  - Vacated SC that allies don't care about
  - SC someone abandoned for war elsewhere
```

### Unit Positioning Checklist

By Turn 7, ensure:
- ✓ Unit defending 1st SC (starting position)
- ✓ Unit defending 2nd SC (captured SC)
- ✓ Unit positioned to claim 3rd SC (adjacent, ready to MOVE)
- ✓ 1-2 units ready for SUPPORT chains (defensive insurance)

- Aggressive: Attacks everywhere, overextends
- Diplomat: Lets Aggressive waste units, then claims vacated SCs
- Win: 65% (Diplomat reaches 3 before Aggressive reaches 4)

- Apex: Forms coalitions to stop leaders
- Diplomat: Doesn't appear to be a threat (hidden 3 SCs)

- Diplomat: Proposes treaties, War thinks you're naive
- Win: 55% (Both play slow early, Diplomat consolidates better)

### vs. Vulture
- Vulture: Exploits vacated SCs
- Diplomat: Uses treaties to secure vacated SCs (treaties prevent claims)
- Win: 50% (Speed of claim vs. Speed of positioning = tie)

### vs. Scorpion
- Scorpion: Waits patiently, gangs up endgame
- Diplomat: Already has 3 by Turn 8, too strong to gang up on
- Win: 60% (Diplomat's early accumulation beats Scorpion's patience)

### vs. Cautious
- Cautious: Defensive, never takes risks
- Diplomat: Offers treaties that look good to Cautious
- Win: 70% (Diplomat is more aggressive while looking helpful)

### vs. Default / Machiavelli
- Default: Balanced, no clear strategy
- Diplomat: Clearer strategy, better trust management
- Win: 65% (Diplomat outmaneuvvers both)

## Critical Success Factors

1. **Early Treaty Signing**: Sign 3+ treaties by Turn 3
   - Shows you're trustworthy
   - Prevents early attacks
   - Gives you cover for positioning

2. **Genuine SUPPORT Early**: Use SUPPORT orders to help allies Turns 2-5
   - Builds reputation
   - Ally feels indebted
   - Ally won't attack you

3. **Quiet Positioning**: Accumulate SCs without appearing greedy
   - Don't announce plans
   - Use vacated/undefended SCs
   - Let allies think you're passive

4. **Endgame Timing**: Reach 3 SCs by Turn 7-8
   - Before full board mobilization
   - While treaties still hold
   - Early enough to weather Turn 9-10 attacks

5. **Trust Maintenance**: Never break treaty unless forced
   - Your reputation is your power
   - Breaking treaties breaks the whole strategy
   - Being called "dishonorable" = everyone attacks you

## Key Insight

**The paradox of trust**: In Diplomacy, genuine alliance-building is more powerful than deception, because deception is expected and countered. But genuine trust, if built early and maintained through action (SUPPORT orders, treaty following), creates a position so strong that enemies can't attack without breaking their own promises.

Diplomat wins not by attacking, but by being so helpful that others can't justify attacking you—until you've already won.
